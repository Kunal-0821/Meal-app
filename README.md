# 🍽️ **Meal Finder App**

GitHub link - https://github.com/Kunal-0821/Meal-app.git

## ✨ **Overview**

The **Meal Finder App** is a **single-page web application** that allows users to **search for meals**, view **meal details**, and manage a list of **favorite meals**. The app integrates with **TheMealDB API** to fetch meal data, including images, names, and cooking instructions. 

With real-time meal suggestions, a favorite management system, and **persistent data storage** using **localStorage**, this app ensures a seamless and interactive experience for users.

---

## 🚀 **Features**

- **🔍 Search Meals**: Type into the search bar to get **real-time meal suggestions** based on your input.
- **📖 View Meal Details**: Click on a meal to see detailed information like **photo**, **name**, and **cooking instructions**.
- **❤️ Add/Remove Favorites**: Easily add meals to your favorites list, and remove them when you no longer want them in your list.
- **🔒 Persistent Favorites**: Favorite meals are saved in **localStorage**, meaning they persist across sessions, even after **browser refreshes** or **restarts**.
- **📱 Responsive Design**: The app is **mobile-friendly** and adapts to both **desktop** and **mobile devices**.

---

## 💻 **Technologies Used**

- **Vanilla JavaScript**: No external libraries or frameworks for JavaScript; the app uses **pure client-side code**.
- **TheMealDB API**: A free API that provides meal data (meal name, image, instructions).
- **HTML5/CSS3**: For the basic structure and styling of the app with **hover effects** and **responsive layout**.
- **localStorage**: For storing **favorite meals** so they persist across sessions (even after refreshing the page).

📖 Usage
🔎 Search for a Meal: Start typing the name of any meal in the search box. The app will automatically fetch and display a list of meal suggestions.
🍴 View Meal Details: Click on any meal from the list to see more information about it, including a photo and cooking instructions.
❤️ Add to Favorites: If you like a meal, click the "❤️" button to add it to your favorites list.
⭐ View Favorites: Click the "My Favorites" button to view all your favorite meals. You can also remove meals from the list.
🔒 Persistent Favorites: Your favorite meals will stay saved even after the browser is closed or refreshed, thanks to localStorage.
